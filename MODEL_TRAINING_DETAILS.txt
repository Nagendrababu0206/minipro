PARKVISION MODEL TRAINING DETAILS
=================================

CHECKPOINT NOW PRESENT LOCALLY
------------------------------
A local YOLOv8s checkpoint is present at models/best.pt. It was downloaded
from the public Hugging Face repository dronefreak/pklot-yolov8s. The model
card reports that it was fine-tuned on PKLot. This training provenance comes
from the publisher; ParkVision has not independently audited its training run.

The checkpoint reports these classes when loaded with Ultralytics:

  0: vacant
  1: occupied

ParkVision predicts the state of a parking bay. It is not a vehicle make/model
classifier and does not have separate classes for car, motorcycle, van, bus,
vehicle color, or license plate. Vehicles may be visible within occupied-bay
training examples, but the model output is the bay's occupancy class.

VERIFICATION PERFORMED
----------------------
- Loaded models/best.pt successfully in the project virtual environment.
- Confirmed class names: vacant and occupied.
- Ran inference on the model publisher's PKLot showcase image.
- Adapter output: 272 detected bays, 169 VACANT and 103 OCCUPIED.
- The temporary source image and generated annotation were removed after the
  smoke test.

This is a compatibility smoke test on one publisher-provided example. It is
not an independent accuracy evaluation and does not prove performance on a
different parking lot or camera. Test representative, labelled images before
making accuracy claims.
